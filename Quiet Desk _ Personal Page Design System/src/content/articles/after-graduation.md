i have about fourteen months left. that's not nothing. it's also not enough.

i've been treating it like a deadline — the way i treat every deadline — which means i'm aware of it constantly and doing very little about it. i wake up, it's there. i go to sleep, it's there. fourteen months. then what.

## the part i can't figure out

everyone seems to have a plan. or at least they perform having a plan convincingly enough that i can't tell the difference.

my plan is: finish the degree. try to not fail the compilers class. maybe get better at cooking something other than kebabs. these aren't answers to "what are you doing after graduation." these are just things that are happening.

i applied to a few internships. i went to the career fair and felt immediately out of place in a room full of people in business casual holding printed resumes. i have a pdf resume. it's fine. it has my github on it. i didn't print it.

i got a phone screen. i practiced leetcode for two weeks until i started dreaming in recursion, then stopped when it started making me feel hollow. i don't know if that's giving up or being sane. both, maybe.

## what i actually want

this is the part i don't say out loud: i want to work on something that feels like it matters to the people using it. not "changes the world" — that phrasing makes me want to lie down. just something that's actually useful, where i can see the person on the other end.

i don't know if that's a job description. maybe it's a value, which is different.

i've been building small things — the projects in the sidebar. right now the only real user is usually me, except for a game i built where the other user was my project partner, which feels less like a user base and more like a hostage situation with grading criteria.

that's probably a data point.

## what i'm asking, i think

if you graduated in the last few years and you figured something out — not a trick, not a linkedin post, something real — i'd genuinely like to know what you did with the uncertainty. not the outcome. the uncertainty.

my email is in the sidebar.
