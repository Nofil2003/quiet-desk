the dorm kitchen has one working burner and a smoke detector with incredibly low standards. i've learned more from it than from most of my classes.

**olive oil burns at a lower temperature than you think.** everyone says this and no one believes it until they've set off the smoke detector at 11pm while their floor-mates are trying to sleep. medium heat. always.

**pasta water should actually be salty.** not "a little salt." like, aggressively salty. the kind of salty where you taste the water and think "this is too salty" — that's when it's right. i made pasta every week for four months before someone told me this and it changed everything.

**you will be more patient with the microwave than you will with any other process in your life.** i have waited eight minutes for a baked potato without once checking my email. i have never sat with a problem for eight uninterrupted minutes.

**the best time to clean the pan is immediately.** this is true of everything. not just the pan.

**cooking for someone else is completely different from cooking for yourself.** i don't know why. the stakes feel different. i have made perfect scrambled eggs exactly once, for a friend at 1am, without thinking about it. i have never reproduced them. she said they were good. i didn't tell her it was an accident.

*the smoke detector situation got resolved in october. we taped a bag over it. this is definitely not a fire hazard.*
